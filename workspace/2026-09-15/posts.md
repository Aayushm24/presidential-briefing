## OPTION 3, personal-discovery (hook score: 8)

**Conviction:** L3: The AI pacing debate creates longer development windows for application companies to build sustainable advantages before foundation models absorb their features.

**Post:**
I've been tracking AI infrastructure stocks wrong.

For months i thought the rotation to software was about better products.

Turns out it's about better timing.

The AI pacing debate that triggered this week's market move creates formal checks and delayed releases rather than slowing progress. Pacing means OpenAI trains GPT-7 but waits six months for safety reviews before release.

That delay changes everything for application companies.

Previously, foundation model capabilities jumped every 6-12 months with minimal warning. Application companies had to assume their advantages would go generic with the next model release.

Under pacing, they get protected development windows.

Six extra months for companies like Superhuman, Notion, and Cursor to compound their lead using current capabilities. Six months to build deeper integration, better user experiences, stronger network effects.

Infrastructure companies face the opposite dynamic. Chips become standard faster than applications get absorbed, so hardware advantage erodes first.

That's why the market moved so decisively toward software companies this week. SentinelOne +15.8%. CrowdStrike +15.4%. NVIDIA -2.8%.

I think the market sees what most builders don't. Pacing turns AI from a hardware arms race into a software endurance race.

The teams building the stickiest products during capability pauses will own the usage when capabilities jump forward again.

At Atlan, we're using this window to go deeper on agent workflow integration rather than betting on the next model upgrade.

What's your team building that gets stronger with time instead of obsolete with the next model release?
